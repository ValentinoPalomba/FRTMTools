# FRTMTools audit reports

## Register

product

## Users

Technical teams reviewing mobile packages and managers reading audit summaries.
The HTML must support both a quick overview and detailed inspection of evidence.

## Product Purpose

Present measured package sizes, components, duplicates, build checks and candidate
security advisories in portable offline HTML reports generated by the CLI.

## Brand Personality

Clean, professional and clear, as requested by the user.

## Anti-references

Decorative gradients, oversized rounding and dense walls of technical prose.
Removing graphs is contrary to the user's request: charts remain central.

## Design Principles

- Lead with measured facts and significant findings.
- Retain charts, filters, sorting and downloadable reports.
- Keep detailed evidence accessible without overwhelming the overview.
- Maintain a consistent format across applications and platforms.
- Omit methodology/source-list sections and translation data, as requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, labelled controls, readable contrast, textual chart
legends and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on colour alone for severity.
